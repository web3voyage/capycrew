02 — The member loop

Make your
next move.

Three small actions keep the club useful: complete a mission, claim a proof of participation, and help choose what ships next.

Table 2.1 — The first three missions, as designed
MissionWhat it asksProofStatus
01 — Shape a district Choose the mood for CapyCity's next block and leave one sentence for the builders. +1 city proof Opens with member accounts
02 — Pass the signal Share the latest CapyCity broadcast with someone who would make the city better. +1 signal proof Opens with member accounts
03 — Bring a collaborator Invite a creator to propose a drop, a sound, or a place in the next district. Planned Opens after launch

These are the first repeatable rituals of CapyCrew. They are designed to take minutes, not a full-time job. Missions open once member accounts ship; until then this is the published design, not a live scoreboard.

Useful things,
when ready.

Future plans are deliberately staged. Each one ships only after the member loop above is working and documented.

  1. 01 CapyCity districtsNext chapter Explore themed blocks with stories, broadcasts, and member-led missions. Target: after Genesis launch.
  2. 02 Digital wearablesNext chapter Collect clothing and accessories that give your Capy a living media identity. Target: prototype.
  3. 03 Creator collaborationsExploring Open a path for artists, musicians, and designers to build official CapyCrew drops. Needs: a contributor model.
  4. 04 CapyCity eventsExploring Bring the club offline through showcases, pop-ups, and small creative gatherings. Needs: local partners.
  5. 05 Interactive worldExploring A lightweight browser world where members can display their Capy and discover stories. Needs: a technical brief.
  6. 06 Community grantsExploring A transparent pool for member-led art, music, events, and city improvements. Needs: a treasury policy.

Status note. Planned features are directional, not promises. Eligibility, timing, and availability will be published before any claim, sale, or launch.5
