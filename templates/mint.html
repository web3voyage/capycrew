03 — How to mint

Four steps
to a Capy.

The mint button always carries the next step: connect, switch network, verify, then mint.

{#- dispatch.js reveals .route-list li itself and fills --route-progress down the spine, so the items carry no data-reveal of their own. -#}
  1. 01 Connect and verify Connect a browser wallet and sign the verification message. If the wallet is on the wrong chain, the portal offers the switch to {{ mint_chain_name }} first.
  2. 02 Choose up to five Pick a quantity between one and five Genesis NFTs. Maximum 5 public mints per wallet.
  3. 03 Confirm one transaction Click Mint now, then confirm the transaction and its payment in your wallet. The receipt links straight to the explorer.
  4. 04 Wait for the revealAfter close Metadata is pre-shuffled before upload and revealed after minting closes.
