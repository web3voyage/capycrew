Quick summary

We do not require an account, we do not ask for your seed phrase or private key, and we do not sell personal information.

The public site can be browsed without connecting a wallet. If you connect a wallet, your public wallet address and related onchain eligibility information may be processed to show mint status. Your browser may temporarily store a verification marker so interface state works across page views.

!
Important

Blockchain transactions and wallet addresses are generally public and cannot be erased from an immutable chain. Privacy rights over onchain records are limited by that technical reality.

01 / Scope

Who this policy covers.

This policy applies to the CapyCrew website and related web interfaces controlled by CapyCrew. It does not apply to third-party wallets, block explorers, marketplaces, social networks, hosting providers, analytics services operated by others, or independent websites reached through links.

If you interact with those services, their own privacy policies control their processing. Always review them before connecting a wallet or sharing information.

03 / How information is used

Limited, service-focused purposes.

We use information to provide the website and mint interface; verify that you control a connected wallet when signature verification is requested; read public contract state; display transaction links; secure and debug systems; comply with law; and respond to you. We do not use personal information for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

We do not sell personal information as that term is commonly understood, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

04 / Legal bases

Why processing is permitted.

Where the GDPR applies, processing may rely on: performance of a service you request; legitimate interests in operating and securing our site, balanced against your rights; consent, where required for optional cookies or communications; and legal obligation, where retention or disclosure is mandatory.

You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis, but withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.

05 / Wallet data & signatures

Proof is not permission.

Connecting a wallet makes its public address available to the site. Where signature verification is used, we request a human-readable personal_sign message identifying CapyCrew and the purpose. Signing proves control of the wallet at that moment; it does not authorize a transfer or grant access to your funds.

Never approve a message or transaction you do not understand. A seed phrase should never be entered into the CapyCrew website. If you believe you interacted with a spoofed domain, disconnect immediately and seek support through official channels.

You can disconnect through your wallet, clear site data, close your browser tab, or use a fresh wallet address for future sessions.

06 / Cookies & local storage

Minimal browser storage.

The current website does not use advertising cookies. It uses browser session storage for a short-lived interface marker and may use strictly necessary storage or cookies for security, load balancing, and basic functionality. Hosting providers may set technical cookies necessary to deliver the service.

Your browser allows you to block, delete, or clear site data. Doing so may reset preferences or require reconnecting a wallet. We will provide notice and obtain any consent required before adding non-essential tracking technologies.

07 / Sharing

Only what operations require.

We may share limited information with service providers that host the site, transmit RPC requests, provide analytics, email, security, audit, or professional services under contractual confidentiality and purpose limitations. Public blockchain data is inherently visible to anyone. We may also disclose information to respond to legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or complete a corporate transaction, subject to applicable law.

08 / Data retention

Kept only as long as needed.

Session-storage markers normally disappear when your browser session ends. Technical logs are retained only for a reasonable operational and security period, typically no longer than 12 months unless required longer. Support communications are retained while handling your matter and then according to legal, accounting, and limitation requirements. Onchain records cannot be deleted because they exist on public networks outside our control.

09 / Security

Safeguards and limits.

We use administrative and technical measures appropriate to risk, including transport encryption, least privilege, dependency maintenance, monitoring, and incident response practices. No online service is perfectly secure. Do not send private keys, seed phrases, or unnecessary personal information to us.

10 / Your rights

Choices and controls.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, port data, withdraw consent, opt out of targeted advertising or sale/sharing, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents may additionally request disclosure about collection and may not be discriminated against for exercising rights.

To make a request, contact privacy@capycrew.com. We verify requests before responding and may permit an authorized agent only with proof of authorization. We aim to respond within the time required by applicable law. Because we generally do not maintain a persistent account profile tied to identity, our ability to locate data may be limited.

11 / Children

Not directed to minors.

CapyCrew is not directed to children under 16, and digital-asset features are not intended for users below the legal age in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it where technically possible.

12 / International transfers

Global infrastructure.

Information may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy findings, standard contractual clauses, or other lawful transfer mechanisms, together with additional technical controls where needed.

13 / Changes

Dated updates.

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. For material changes, we will provide reasonable notice through the website or another appropriate channel.

14 / Contact

Reach the privacy team.

Email privacy@capycrew.com for questions, complaints, or privacy requests. If a concern is unresolved where required, EU/UK residents may contact their competent data protection authority.

This policy is provided as project documentation and does not constitute legal advice.
