Abstract

CapyCrew is an animation-first NFT universe built around a simple idea: the internet is better when participation feels human.

The project combines collectible characters, a shared setting called CapyCity, and a planned memecoin called $MELLOW. The site, collection, and community are the first public districts. New features ship only when they are real, documented, and safe to use.

01
Scope

This document describes the current product and a planned token model. It does not offer securities, guarantee a token generation event, or promise market performance.

10 / Security & operations

Verify first,
always.

The project will publish canonical contract addresses, deployment transactions, metadata roots, and known-issue notices. Operational controls should include least-privilege access, timelocked upgrades where feasible, incident disclosure, and independent review before high-risk changes.

!
User protection

CapyCrew will never ask for a seed phrase or private key. A wallet signature proves control; it never authorizes token movement unless you approve a separate transaction.

11 / Risks & legal

Know the edges.

Digital assets are volatile and experimental. Smart contracts, token launches, wallets, bridges, third-party services, phishing, market illiquidity, and changing regulations carry real risk. Tokens and NFTs may lose all value. Never share a seed phrase, verify the contract and URL, and only use funds you can afford to lose.
