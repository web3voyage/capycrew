02 — Abstract

The internet is better
when it feels human.

What this document is, what it describes, and what it deliberately does not promise.

{#- .columns is the only real prose measure in this design: two columns with a rule, set in the serif. Running copy that is not in .columns, .notice, .band-note or .figure-note has no width cap and runs the full shell. -#}

CapyCrew is an animation-first NFT universe built around a simple idea: the internet is better when participation feels human.

The project combines collectible characters, a shared setting called CapyCity, and a product loop built around profiles, galleries, monthly missions, rewards, polls, and a public activity feed. The site, collection, and community are the first public districts. New features ship only when they are real, documented, and safe to use.

Scope

This document describes the current product and a planned token model. It does not offer securities, guarantee a token generation event, or promise market performance.

03 — The world

Stay mellow.
Build the signal.

The setting, and the format the characters are published in.

CapyCity is a soft-edged onchain setting for characters, moving image, and collective experiments. Every release should stand on its own while adding another piece to the wider story.

Characters with a media identity

Animated CapyCrew NFTs are the membership layer. Each token can carry a still cover, a video animation_url, metadata, and access rights that expand only when the relevant product is live.

  • Standard. ERC-721A
  • Format. Image + video
  • Storage target. IPFS / Arweave

Principle

Playful on the surface, specific underneath. No artificial urgency and no promises that depend on a future launch.

04 — The signal loop

Discover → collect
→ participate → shape.

How a member arrives, and what the first focused web app has to carry once they do.

{#- .notes-list keeps its markers, so an
    numbers itself 1-4: the same four steps the old .loop-grid printed as 01-04. -#}
    1. Discover. Find a drop, story, or community moment through official channels.
    2. Collect. Use a compatible wallet and verify the contract before signing.
    3. Participate. Join showcases, holder tooling, and experiments that are actually live.
    4. Shape. Feedback and proposals help decide what deserves the next district.

    The experience is the product

    The first focused web app is designed to give holders a reason to return every month. It is not a collection viewer bolted onto a mint page; it is a member home with a clear loop and visible progress.

    1. Wallet + profile. Connect, verify a Capy, and manage traits, animations, badges, and participation history.
    2. Holder gallery. Inspect characters in a collection-style display with trait metadata and animation previews.
    3. Monthly missions. Design districts, submit concepts, vote on drops, complete challenges, and attend events.
    4. Public activity. Follow districts, drops, contributions, mission completions, and community decisions.

Inside ecosystem & missions / rarity weighting

100M tokens, weighted by tier.

06 — Distribution & unlocks

Transparent timing,
no silent supply.

When supply could move, and what the token would coordinate if it ever does.

The numbers below are a planning envelope, not a commitment. Final supply, float, allocation, vesting, and launch mechanics require legal, technical, community, and demand review. The design prioritizes low initial float, milestone-gated distribution, and no unlock shock larger than the product can support.

Coordination, not speculation

  1. Mission coordination. If launched, the token may help coordinate optional campaigns, creator challenges, or district events.
  2. Creator access. Approved contributors may use it for transparent collaboration budgets or campaign credits.
  3. Governance signal. It may signal preferences over treasury proposals or future themes where the decision is genuinely binding.
  4. Holding incentives. Any lock or reward mechanism must be funded by real product activity, not unsustainable inflation.

$CAPY is not designed as payment for goods unless expressly stated at checkout, and it does not create a claim against CapyCrew revenue.

Reading the table

Every phase is a gate rather than a date. Nothing circulates until the gate above it has passed.

09 — Game concept

CapyCity:
After Hours.

The interactive layer, filed as a concept and ranked behind the member product.

A lightweight browser world where members explore districts, display their Capy, discover stories, and turn monthly missions into visible city changes. The game is an extension of the membership experience—not a speculative promise or a requirement for the NFT to have value as a collectible.

  1. Explore. Move through Street, Studio, Arcade, and Harbor using a small, readable browser map.
  2. Make. Submit outfits, music, district props, and story fragments through moderated creator prompts.
  3. Earn. Complete challenges to receive non-transferable badges, art unlocks, early access, or event priority.
  4. Shape. Community votes and treasury grants decide which district ideas become permanent content.

Animation concept — “Signal Pulse”

A 12-second loop for the game and social drops: Capy enters from frame left, the card emits a cyan-orange pulse, a district marker blooms underfoot, three trait callouts lock into place, and the camera cuts into the next route. Motion is restrained, readable, and reusable across profile headers, missions, and event screens.

10 — Architecture & operations

Document what
is live.

What gets published as it deploys, and the controls around it.

Contracts, network details, metadata endpoints, claim mechanics, and audit status will be linked here as they are deployed. Until then, treat integrations as prototypes and use only links published on the official domain.

Verify first, always

The project will publish canonical contract addresses, deployment transactions, metadata roots, and known-issue notices. Operational controls should include least-privilege access, timelocked upgrades where feasible, incident disclosure, and independent review before high-risk changes.

Canonical only

If a contract address did not come from the official domain or an official channel, it is not ours.

Confirm before signing2

Contracts — Published per deploymentAudits — Before material launchCanonical links — capycrew.com only

User protection. CapyCrew will never ask for a seed phrase or private key. A wallet signature proves control; it never authorizes token movement unless you approve a separate transaction.

11 — Risks

Know the edges.

The parts of this that can go wrong, stated plainly.

Digital assets are volatile and experimental. Smart contracts, token launches, wallets, bridges, third-party services, phishing, market illiquidity, and changing regulations carry real risk. Tokens and NFTs may lose all value. Never share a seed phrase, verify the contract and URL, and only use funds you can afford to lose.
