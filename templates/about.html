01 / What is CapyCrew?

Characters with somewhere to go.

CapyCrew is a 10,000-piece collectible character project built on the Robinhood Chain. Each Capy is a digital identity and membership pass into CapyCity, an evolving universe shaped by character art, fashion, animation, and community culture.

The project is designed around long-term participation rather than short-term speculation. CapyCrew gives people a shared world to explore, contribute to, and make their own.

02 / Utility

Useful reasons to stay curious.

  • Digital membership: access to CapyCrew community channels, announcements, and holder activities.
  • Digital identity: a unique character, style, and role within the CapyCity universe.
  • Community experiences: creative challenges, collaborations, events, and community-led experiments.
  • Holder rewards: future digital collectibles, airdrops, merchandise, and partner benefits as project milestones are reached.
  • Interactive expansion: a foundation for stories, city districts, games, and other digital experiences.
  • Community influence: opportunities to help shape future themes, collaborations, features, and creative direction.
03 / The direction

Build the city together.

CapyCity grows in chapters: characters, districts, broadcasts, drops, and experiments. The route is guided by useful deliverables and an open, welcoming culture rather than hype.

CapyCrew NFTs are collectibles and access passes to the CapyCrew ecosystem. They do not represent equity, ownership in a company, or a guaranteed financial return. Future utilities depend on development, partnerships, and community participation.
