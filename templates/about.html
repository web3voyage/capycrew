CAPYCREW / THE PROJECT

A creative
internet club.

CapyCrew is a collectible character world where your Capy becomes your identity, your membership pass, and your way to help shape CapyCity.

01 / What is CapyCrew?

Characters with somewhere to go.

CapyCrew is a 10,000-piece collectible character project built on the Robinhood Chain. Each Capy is a digital identity and membership pass into CapyCity, an evolving universe shaped by character art, fashion, animation, games, and community culture.

The product is the experience around the collectible: a profile, a holder gallery, monthly missions, community polls, a public activity feed, and a growing library of districts, drops, badges, and events.

02 / The MVP

Give holders a reason to return.

  • Wallet + Capy profile: connect a wallet, verify a Capy, and manage traits, animations, badges, and participation history.
  • Holder gallery: inspect characters in a collection-style display with trait metadata, animation previews, and archive navigation.
  • Monthly CapyCity missions: design a district, submit an outfit or music idea, vote on a drop, complete a challenge, or attend an event.
  • Rewards: digital badges, exclusive artwork, early access, merchandise discounts, and event access when each reward is live and documented.
  • Community polls: help choose themes, collaborations, district directions, and future character drops.
  • Public activity feed: follow new districts, drops, member contributions, mission completions, and community decisions.
03 / The direction

Build the city together.

CapyCity grows in chapters: Street, Studio, Arcade, Harbor, and the places members invent next. Each district can carry its own characters, media, missions, collaborations, and event format.

CapyCrew NFTs are collectibles and access passes to the CapyCrew ecosystem. They do not represent equity, ownership in a company, or a guaranteed financial return. Features are staged, conditional, and subject to product, partnership, legal, and community review.

04 / Game concept

CapyCity: After Hours.

A lightweight browser adventure where your Capy walks through themed districts, takes on monthly missions, collects wearables, and leaves visible traces of community activity.

  • Core loop: explore a district → accept a mission → make or discover something → earn a badge or unlock → return for the next chapter.
  • District design: Street is the social plaza, Studio is the creator lab, Arcade is the challenge floor, and Harbor is the listening/event space.
  • Animation language: a 12-second “signal pulse” loop—Capy steps into frame, the card lights up, a district marker blooms, then the camera cuts to the next route.
