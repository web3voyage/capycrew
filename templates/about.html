02 — Notes

Characters with
somewhere to go.

What the project is, and what the member product has to carry before any of it counts as shipped.

{#- .columns is a real two-column measure with a column rule; .margin-note sits beside it and goes sticky above 1100px. h3 inside .columns is re-set as a mono forest subhead, so it does not need a class. -#}

CapyCrew is a 10,000-piece collectible character project built on {{ mint_chain_name }}. Each Capy is a digital identity and a membership pass into CapyCity, an evolving universe shaped by character art, fashion, animation, games, and community culture.

The product is the experience around the collectible: a profile, a holder gallery, monthly missions, community polls, a public activity feed, and a growing library of districts, drops, badges, and events.

The MVP we are building

This is the member product being built, not a list of features that are live today. The Crew Hub publishes each one as it ships, and the build log is dated.

  • Wallet and Capy profile. Connect a wallet, verify a Capy, and manage traits, animations, badges, and participation history.
  • Holder gallery. Inspect characters in a collection-style display with trait metadata, animation previews, and archive navigation.
  • Monthly CapyCity missions. Design a district, submit an outfit or music idea, vote on a drop, complete a challenge, or attend an event.
  • Rewards. Digital badges, exclusive artwork, early access, merchandise discounts, and event access — each one documented when it is live.
  • Community polls. Help choose themes, collaborations, district directions, and future character drops.
  • Public build log. Follow new districts, drops, member contributions, mission completions, and community decisions.

04 — Game concept

CapyCity:
After Hours.

A concept study for the interactive layer, filed here so the shape of it is public before any of it is built.

A lightweight browser adventure where your Capy walks through themed districts, takes on monthly missions, collects wearables, and leaves visible traces of community activity.

  • Core loop. Explore a district, accept a mission, make or discover something, earn a badge or an unlock, then return for the next chapter.
  • District design. Street is the social plaza, Studio is the creator lab, Arcade is the challenge floor, and Harbor is the listening and event space.
  • Animation language. A twelve-second signal pulse: the Capy steps into frame, the card lights up, a district marker blooms, then the camera cuts to the next route.

Filed as concept

Nothing on this page is dated, priced, or promised. The game layer sits behind the member product in the running order, and the route on the front page is the one under construction.
